## Approvals, access and the back office

### POL-01 Approvals, delegation and escalation

**Policy:** every transaction is approved by the right person for its risk, never by the person who prepared it.

- Approvals follow the approval matrix in the Control Register. Below ₱50,000 the Finance approver decides; at or above it, the company's Final approver also approves. The line compares the document total including VAT (H1).
- An approver going on leave delegates their rights for set dates; a delegate never approves their own work.
- An approval waiting more than 2 business days escalates to the next approver or the delegate.
- Approvers may approve on company tablets; approvals of ₱50,000 or more need re-authentication.

**Owner:** second owner (Arielle de Leon) · **Controls:** GEN-01, GEN-07, GEN-08 · **Settings:** approval lines, escalation days

### POL-02 User access and mandatory leave

**Policy:** each person has their own account with only the roles their job needs, access is removed the day they leave, and people in key roles take real leave every year.

- Accounts are never shared; every user signs in with MFA.
- The Admin makes access changes; changes to financial roles need the second owner's approval; the Admin never grants roles to themselves. Every permission change alerts the second owner.
- Assigning a conflicting pair of roles warns, and the owner's acceptance is logged.
- The admin-owner (Joseph de Leon) has two logins: an admin login with no financial rights and an owner login with no approver role. (new)
- Auditor accounts are read-only, time-limited and have their own MFA; their exports are logged. (new)
- A leaver's access is removed on their last working day; a role change removes the old roles the same day.
- The second owner reviews every user and role, and the admin activity log, each quarter.
- The Encoder, Accountant and each Cashier take at least 5 consecutive business days of leave a year while their backup does the work. (new)

**Owner:** second owner (Arielle de Leon) · **Controls:** GEN-02 to GEN-06, GEN-09, GEN-11, ITC-01, ITC-09

### POL-03 Back-office service levels and the monthly close

**Policy:** the accounting team records transactions promptly and closes each month on time, so the books are always current.

- Invoice Records and collections are recorded within 1 business day.
- Supplier invoices are recorded within 3 business days of receipt (starting value).
- Each month closes within the close calendar. In close, only the Accountant posts, and only adjusting entries. (new link)
- Only the Final approver closes or reopens a period; reopening needs a reason and alerts the second owner. (new link)
- Every balance-sheet account is reconciled monthly, with support and a second person's review; the owners receive a monthly review pack. (new link)
- Other targets are set after a 1 to 3 month baseline from go-live and reviewed quarterly.

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** SAL-02, CSH-04, CLS-01 to CLS-04, CLS-10 · **Settings:** recording days, close days

## Customers, credit and government contracts

### POL-04 Credit limits, credit terms and exempt customers

**Policy:** we sell on credit only to customers we have checked, up to a limit they can pay, and we treat a sale as exempt only with proof.

- A new business customer gives a credit application with its TIN, registration papers and trade references; the Finance approver approves the customer.
- Credit limits are set and changed only by the company's Final approver; standard credit terms are 30 days (starting value).
- Government agencies follow the terms in their contract or agency purchase order (POL-17).
- Limits are reviewed every year, and sooner when a customer pays late.
- When a business customer has invoices more than 60 days past due (starting value), new orders are held until the Final approver releases them (sales module).
- An exempt or zero-rated sale needs the customer's valid exemption document on file before the Invoice Record is submitted; VATable, exempt and zero-rated sales are kept separate. (new)

**Owner:** each company's Final approver · **Controls:** SAL-06, SAL-07, SAL-11, TAX-04 · **Settings:** standard credit terms, credit-hold days

### POL-05 Pricing and discounts

**Policy:** prices come from approved price lists, and discounts follow fixed authority tiers.

- List prices are set by the owners and changed only through an approved price list.
- Discounts at quotation follow percentage-off-list tiers held in settings; the percentages are set with the sales module.
- Government bids are priced from the agency's approved budget for the contract and approved by an owner before submission.

**Owner:** owners · **Controls:** SAL-05 · **Settings:** discount tiers (with the sales module)

### POL-06 Returns, credit memos and debit memos

**Policy:** goods come back only with authorization, and every change to what a customer owes is approved and documented.

- A return needs a return authorization and is accepted within 30 days of delivery (starting value), unless the goods are defective.
- Returned goods are inspected; defective or expired stock follows POL-13.
- Credit memos and debit memos follow the ₱50,000 tiers and carry the input-tax phrase.

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** SAL-05, SAL-10 · **Settings:** return window

### POL-07 Bad debts and the allowance

**Policy:** receivables are carried at what we expect to collect, and none is written off without proof of effort and the Final approver's approval.

- The allowance for doubtful accounts uses a provision matrix by aging bucket, with a separate, lower matrix for government agencies; the Accountant (Leslie) updates it quarterly.
- Collection efforts are documented: statements of account, calls, demand letters.
- A write-off needs documented efforts and either an insolvent customer or a balance over 1 year past due (starting value), and the Final approver's approval.
- Written-off balances stay on a recovery list; any later collection is recorded as a recovery.
- Tax deductibility of a write-off follows the accountant's guidance ⚠️.

**Owner:** each company's Final approver · **Controls:** SAL-08, SAL-09 · **Settings:** matrix rates, write-off age

### POL-17 Government contracts and securities (new)

**Policy:** every government contract's withholding, securities and retention are tracked from bid to final release, so no peso owed to us is forgotten.

- Government collections are net of 5% VAT withholding and 1% expanded withholding; each needs its 2307, chased on the "2307 not yet received" list.
- Cash bid and performance securities (5% performance security by default) are tracked per contract until returned; surety bonds and bank guarantees are being explored.
- Warranty retention (1% by default, up to 5% per contract) is tracked per contract to its warranty end date; a release that is overdue raises an alarm.
- Advance payments from an agency, up to 15% of the contract, are accepted only against the bank guarantee the contract requires.
- Revenue is recognized at inspection and acceptance where the contract says so.
- Warranty periods and advance-payment terms follow RA 12009 and its rules as confirmed by the compliance advisor ⚠️.

**Owner:** each company's Final approver · **Controls:** SAL-12, TAX-01 to TAX-03 · **Settings:** default retention rate, default performance security rate

## Cash, checks and e-wallets

### POL-08 Petty cash

**Policy:** small expenses are paid from a fixed fund that always adds up to the same total in cash plus receipts.

- Each company with a Cashier keeps one fund of ₱10,000 (starting value), held by the Cashier as custodian: Merle Tapiador (Agno), Shie Raet (Blasmorisson), Jessa Palaca (Merritmed). Arima and Florminsons keep no fund until a Cashier is named. (new)
- A single payment from the fund is at most ₱1,000 (starting value); anything larger goes through a payment voucher.
- Every payment has a receipt or a signed petty cash voucher.
- The fund is replenished by payment voucher against the receipts when it falls below 30% or at month-end, whichever comes first.
- The Accountant (Leslie) counts the fund by surprise at least once a month.

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** CSH-08 · **Settings:** fund size, single-payment limit

### POL-09 Cash advances and liquidation

**Policy:** cash given to an employee before an expense is accounted for promptly, with receipts and any change returned.

- A cash advance needs a stated purpose and approval by the Finance approver (by payment voucher).
- The employee liquidates within 5 business days after the activity (starting value), with receipts and returned change.
- The liquidation is approved by someone other than the employee.
- No new advance is given while the same employee has one outstanding.
- An advance unliquidated past the deadline raises an alarm; each employee signs a salary-deduction authorization when the advance is given, worded with a labor advisor ⚠️.

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** CSH-08 · **Settings:** liquidation days

### POL-10 Checks

**Policy:** blank checks are locked away, every check is accounted for, and no check is signed blank or made out to cash.

- Blank checks are kept in a locked safe; the Cashier is custodian, and the check numbers are tracked in a check register.
- Checks are issued only against an approved payment voucher; signing follows the bank release matrix.
- Checks are never signed blank and never made payable to "Cash."
- Spoiled checks are marked VOID, kept and recorded in the check register.
- Checks we issued and still outstanding after 90 days are flagged in the bank reconciliation; how they are treated follows the accountant's answer ⚠️.
- Post-dated checks received are logged, kept in the safe and deposited on their date.

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** CSH-06, CSH-07, PAY-03 · **Settings:** outstanding-check days

### POL-11 Collections, deposits and e-wallets

**Policy:** every peso received is acknowledged, recorded by someone other than the person who received it, and banked intact.

- The Cashier issues an acknowledgement receipt for every collection.
- Collections are deposited intact the same or next business day and matched to the deposit slip and bank line; nothing is paid out of collections.
- The Encoder (Lorelai) records each collection within 1 business day and applies it to specific Invoice Records.
- Money that can't be applied is held as an unapplied collection and cleared within 30 days.
- GCash and Maya are company business accounts used for collections only. Their only outflow is a sweep to the company's bank account at least weekly; personal wallets are never used. (new)

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** CSH-01 to CSH-05, CSH-09 · **Settings:** sweep frequency

## Payments, payees and suppliers

### POL-12 Payments, payee details and supplier advances

**Policy:** money leaves only through an approved payment voucher and a two-person bank release, to a payee whose bank details we have verified.

- Every payment has an approved payment voucher backed by a three-way match or other source document; payor withholding is computed on the voucher.
- New payees and any change to bank details are approved by the Finance approver and confirmed by calling a number already on file, never one given in the request.
- Release at the bank follows the bank release matrix; the voucher number goes in the bank's reference field; nobody releases a payment they prepared.
- No one approves or releases a payment to themselves; related-party payments are released by the second owner only.
- In the sole proprietorships (Blasmorisson, Merritmed), with one bank signatory for now, the Accountant prepares as maker and the owner releases; payments of ₱50,000 or more and owner drawings are approved in the app first. (new)
- Jose Mahatma de Leon may stand in for the second owner on payments of ₱50,000 or more to existing verified payees, within the cap the second owner sets in writing, and reports each use the same day.
- Splitting a payment to stay under the ₱50,000 line is not allowed; the split-payment alarm watches for two or more payments to one payee within 7 days.
- An advance to a supplier needs the supplier's proforma invoice and an approved purchase order attached before its voucher is submitted; advances not matched to a shipment within 60 days are flagged. (new)

**Owner:** second owner (Arielle de Leon) · **Controls:** PUR-02, PAY-01 to PAY-10 · **Settings:** approval and release lines, alternate releaser's cap, split-payment window, advance flag days

### POL-18 Imports and foreign currency (new)

**Policy:** an import's full cost is captured on one approved landed cost sheet, and input VAT is claimed only from the documents the law accepts.

- Own-name imports: import VAT is claimed only with the import entry and the customs receipt; the commercial invoice supports cost, never input VAT.
- Door-to-door imports: input VAT only from the forwarder's VAT Invoice ⚠️ (pending written confirmation).
- Each import's freight, insurance, duties and brokerage go on a landed cost sheet, approved under the ₱50,000 tiers, before the goods' cost is final.
- Peso advances paid before shipment are not revalued ⚠️ (pending written confirmation); an open USD balance is revalued at month-end at the BSP reference rate, and a rate is never edited once used.

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** PUR-03, PAY-09, PAY-10, CLS-07 · **Settings:** advance flag days

## Inventory and fixed assets

### POL-13 Inventory counts and stock write-offs

**Policy:** stock is counted by people who don't look after it, and any stock removed from the books is approved and explained.

- A full physical count at every year-end, plus a monthly spot count of high-value items (starting value), done by staff independent of the warehouse custodian.
- Count differences are investigated before the books are adjusted; adjustments follow the ₱50,000 tiers.
- Damaged, expired or obsolete stock is written down to net realizable value at least at year-end, and written off with the ₱50,000 tiers.
- Written-off stock is disposed of with a witness and a disposal record.

**Owner:** Finance approver (Margaret DL Laforteza) · **Controls:** CLS-06 · **Settings:** spot-count frequency, write-off tiers

### POL-16 Fixed assets and custody items

**Policy:** anything used for more than a year and costing ₱20,000 or more is a fixed asset, kept in the app's register and depreciated monthly; portable or theft-prone items below ₱20,000 are expensed but tagged and kept on a custody list.

- **Depreciation:** straight-line, monthly, from the month after purchase. Useful lives: vehicles 5 years; warehouse and office equipment 5; computers and tablets 3; furniture 5; buildings 25; leasehold improvements the shorter of the lease or 5 years; land never ⚠️.
- **Each asset records:** tag number, description, company, class, date bought, cost, supplier and invoice, location, person responsible, useful life, depreciation to date; titles and registration papers attached, in the owning company's name.
- **Custody list:** laptops, tablets, phones, power tools and similar items below ₱20,000, with tag number and the person responsible.
- **Yearly count:** every fixed asset and custody item is checked against the register at year-end and signed by two people; differences are investigated.
- **Disposal:** selling, scrapping or writing off an asset needs the company's Final approver; any gain or loss is posted (7130, 7220).
- **Existing assets:** rebuilt into the register from purchase invoices, registration papers and titles; where cost can't be found, the external accountant sets a reasonable figure in writing ⚠️. The register and custody list are rebuilt in early December 2026, so every asset loads individually in the December 31, 2026 opening (Opening Balances Plan; Q1).

**Owner:** Accountant (Leslie) maintains; Finance approver reviews · **Controls:** CLS-12 · **Settings:** capitalization threshold (S46), useful lives (S47)

## Related parties, group charges and shared staff

### POL-14 Related-party and intercompany transactions

**Policy:** dealings with owners, their relatives and group companies are recorded openly, priced fairly and approved by someone independent.

- Related parties are the owners, their relatives and every group company; each is flagged in the partner records.
- Related-party transactions are priced as they would be with an outsider, and the basis is documented.
- Payments to the admin-owner or another related party are released by the second owner only.
- An intercompany transaction is recorded in both companies as a linked pair; either owner may release a linked payment below ₱50,000.
- Charges between group companies, such as rent and vehicle use, follow a written agreement at market rates and are invoiced monthly; each vehicle is titled to one company. (new)
- Owner loans and owner advances are recorded separately and never netted; in the sole proprietorships, the owner's withdrawals are drawings. (new)
- A person paid by more than one group company is paid separately by each under a time-share contract, and the shares total no more than 100%. (new)
- Intercompany balances are reconciled every month and must net to zero.
- Any BIR reporting of related-party transactions follows the accountant's guidance ⚠️.

**Owner:** second owner (Arielle de Leon) · **Controls:** PAY-04, CLS-05, CLS-11, GEN-12

## Records

### POL-15 Record retention and legal hold

**Policy:** financial records are kept for 10 years, never destroyed early, and frozen whenever an audit or dispute needs them.

- App records and their evidence are kept for 10 years and never hard-deleted (pending the accountant's confirmation).
- Paper originals are kept too: invoice booklets including spoiled Invoices, 2307s, signed delivery receipts, deposit slips and bank statements.
- Paper is stored by company and year in a locked, dry place; photographs in the app are evidence, not a replacement for originals the BIR requires.
- A legal hold, set by an owner, stops any archiving or disposal while an audit or dispute is open.
- Personal data kept beyond 10 years, or not needed for these records, follows the privacy advisor's guidance ⚠️.

**Owner:** Data Protection Officer (Arielle de Leon) with the Accountant (Leslie) · **Controls:** ITC-02, ITC-05, ITC-08 · **Settings:** retention years
